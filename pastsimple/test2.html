When to use Past Simple
Choose the sentence that correctly uses Past Simple — or identify why it's used.
📋 Slide 3
Past Simple describes completed facts or events in the past that are not connected to the present. Use it when the action is fully over and, often, when a specific past time is implied or stated.
Examples: Neil Armstrong walked on the Moon in 1969.  ·  She called you 5 minutes ago.

Spelling rules for –ed
Three rules: verbs ending in -e, verbs ending in -y, and consonant doubling. Choose the correctly spelled form.
📋 Slides 5 – 6
–e ending: add -d only  ·  code → coded  ·  agree → agreed
Vowel + y: add -ed  ·  play → played
Consonant + y: change to -ied  ·  study → studied
CVC (short stressed syllable): double consonant + -ed  ·  stop → stopped
Multi-syllable, stress on last: double + -ed  ·  transfer → transferred
