PART A · THE LOGIC IN ONE SCREEN

★ Recap of the lesson

Four criteria · 25% eachTask Response · Coherence & Cohesion · Lexical Resource · Grammatical Range & Accuracy. Language is about half of the marks; the rest is technique.
Five question typesOpinion: state and support your view · Discussion: both views, then your opinion · Advantages / disadvantages: both sides · Causes / solutions: equal weight · Two-part: answer both questions.
The essay mapIntroduction (2–3 sentences) · Body 1 (about 85 words) · Body 2 (about 85 words) · Conclusion (1–2 sentences). Plan 5 · write 30 · check 5 minutes. 250+ words, aim for 270–290.
Body paragraph: four movesPosition → Reason → Example → Link back. Specific example plus reasoning is the jump from band 6 to 7.
LinkingVary the devices; not every sentence needs one. Stay formal (no “all in all”, no “despite of”). Link ideas, not just words. Memorised openers are risky.
What bands 8–9 addFully extended ideas · a nuanced position (concede, then qualify) · precise vocabulary · accurate complex structures · cohesion that comes from meaning, not from a linker on every line.
How to use this page. Work through stations 1–9, press Check Answers, then write in the lab. The writing lab gives instant feedback on length, copying, stock phrases, structure and linking. Compare with the models under Show Answers.
