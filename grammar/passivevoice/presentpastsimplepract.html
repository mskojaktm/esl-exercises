Present Simple Passive

am / is / are + V3 (Past Participle)

Используется для регулярных действий, фактов, процессов и общеизвестных истин.

  • Affirmative: Coffee is grown in Brazil.
  • Negative: Emails are not (aren't) sent on Sundays.
  • Question: Is the museum cleaned every day?

Past Simple Passive

was / were + V3 (Past Participle)

Используется для завершенных действий в прошлом, где исполнитель неизвестен или неважен.

  • Affirmative: The castle was built in 1492.
  • Negative: The documents were not (weren't) found yesterday.
  • Question: Was the window broken by the kids?

Предлог BY

Указывает на одушевленного исполнителя или организацию: The book was written by Orwell.

Предлог WITH

Указывает на инструмент, материал или ингредиент: The soup was seasoned with herbs.

Опущение Агента (Agent Omission)

Агент опускается, если это people, someone, they или если автор очевиден: The thief was arrested (by police - очевидно).

Reading Text 1 • Process & Technology • Present Simple Passive Focus

How Modern Paper Recycling Works

Paper recycling is a critical industrial process by which used paper is converted into new, reusable products. Every day, tons of paper waste are collected from households, offices, and schools. First, the gathered material is sorted into different grades, such as cardboard, newsprint, and office stationery. Contaminants like plastics, glass, and metals are removed manually or with automated separators.

After sorting, the paper is transported to a recycling mill. There, it is mixed with water and chemical solutions in a giant vessel called a pulper. The mixture is heated and chopped into tiny strands of cellulose fibers. This thick liquid is known as pulp. To ensure high quality, the pulp is passed through screens with holes of varying sizes to filter out remaining staples, glue, and tape.

Next, the de-inking phase takes place. Ink particles and sticky substances are detached using air bubbles in a process called flotation. Then, the cleaned pulp is bleached with hydrogen peroxide or chlorine-free agents to enhance whiteness. Finally, the wet pulp is spread onto large moving wire screens. Water is drained out, and the sheet is pressed through heavy heated rollers. Once dried, the new paper is wound onto massive reels and is shipped to manufacturers worldwide.

Reading Text 2 • History & Mystery • Past Simple Passive Focus

The Mystery of the Mary Celeste

The Mary Celeste remains one of the most intriguing maritime mysteries in history. Built in Canada in 1861, the merchant ship was originally named the Amazon. In November 1872, under the command of Captain Benjamin Briggs, the vessel was loaded with over 1,700 barrels of industrial alcohol and was dispatched from New York toward Genoa, Italy.

However, the ship never reached its intended destination. On December 4, 1872, the Mary Celeste was spotted in the Atlantic Ocean by the crew of a British brig, the Dei Gratia. The ship appeared to be drifting aimlessly. When a boarding party was sent to inspect the vessel, a baffling scene was discovered. The ship was fully seaworthy, and its cargo of alcohol was left virtually untouched. Six months’ worth of food and fresh water were stored in the hold.

Yet, not a single person was found on board. The lifeboat was removed, along with the captain’s navigation tools and logbook. No signs of violent struggle were noticed, and the personal belongings of the crew were left behind intact. Over the decades, dozens of theories were proposed by researchers—ranging from sea monster attacks to pirate raids and alcohol vapor explosions. Ultimately, no definitive proof was presented, and the true fate of the passengers was never uncovered.
