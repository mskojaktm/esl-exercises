Task 3: Generate Your Own Examples by Category

Written Production
Using Daniel Pink's classification of the four core regrets, generate your own original examples (from your own life, from people you know, or realistic fictional scenarios). For each category, describe the situation briefly and write 1–2 sentences using I wish or If only.

1. Foundation Regrets (Stability: Health, Education, Finances)

Small choices made early on that fail to build a stable platform for life (e.g., not saving money, neglecting health, not studying hard enough).

2. Boldness Regrets (Taking Chances & Speaking Up)

Moments at a crossroads where you played it safe instead of taking a risk, starting something new, or speaking your mind.

3. Moral Regrets (Doing the Right Thing)

Situations where you faced a choice between right and wrong, chose the wrong path, and still feel bothered by that moral breach.

4. Connection Regrets (Relationships & Reaching Out)

Relationships with family, friends, or colleagues that drifted apart over time because you hesitated to reach out.
Model Examples for Reference:
  • Foundation: "I ignored minor back pain for years while working at a desk. I wish I had started exercising regularly in my twenties."
  • Boldness: "A few years ago, I was offered a job transfer to another country, but I turned it down out of fear. If only I'd taken the chance to live abroad."
  • Moral: "When a classmate was unfairly blamed for a mistake I made, I stayed silent. I wish I'd admitted the truth at the time."
  • Connection: "My closest childhood friend and I stopped calling each other after moving to different cities. If only I had reached out when I first thought of her."
