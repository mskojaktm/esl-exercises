Say · Tell · Speak · Talk

Level B1–B2 · 45–55 minutes · 11 exercises + writing + speaking

Score: 0 / 0

0Warm-up: the key differences5 min

Read the rules. Then try to think of one more example for each verb.

SAY (said)

  • Focus on the words that are spoken.
  • say + words / that-clause: She said (that) she was tired.
  • Person is optional and needs to: He said to me… (not said me)
  • say hello, say sorry, say yes, say a few words

TELL (told)

  • Focus on giving information to someone.
  • tell + person (+ information): She told me she was tired.
  • tell sb (not) to do sth: He told us to wait.
  • tell the truth / a lie / a joke / a story / a secret / the time / the difference

SPEAK (spoke)

  • The ability to use a language, or more formal / one-way speaking.
  • speak English, speak fluently, speak slowly, speak up
  • speak to / with sb: I'd like to speak to the manager.
  • speak at a conference, speak your mind

TALK (talked)

  • A conversation, usually informal and two-way.
  • talk to / with sb about sth: We talked about football.
  • talk business / politics / nonsense / sense
  • talk for hours, small talk

12Writing7 min

Write 80–100 words about a conversation you remember (a family dinner, a job interview, a phone call). Use at least six different expressions with say, tell, speak and talk.

13Speaking: use all four verbs7 min

Work in pairs or with your teacher. Use say, tell, speak and talk correctly in your answers.

TALK
What do you and your friends usually talk about? Is there a topic you never talk about?
TELL
Tell your partner a short story or a joke. Can you tell when someone is lying?
SPEAK
Which languages do you speak? When do you feel nervous speaking English?
SAY
What is the best (or worst) thing anyone has said to you? Do you find it hard to say sorry?
MIX
Describe a time when someone didn't tell you the truth. What did you say to them?
REPORT
Tell your partner what your boss, teacher or parents said to you last week.

Teacher tip: note mistakes with say/tell and give feedback at the end.
