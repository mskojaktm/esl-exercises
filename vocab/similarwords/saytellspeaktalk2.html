Grammar Cheat Sheet & Collocation Guide

SAY

Words / Quotes

Focus on exact words spoken. **No personal object** directly after!

  • Say + (that) + clause:
    He said (that) he was busy.
  • Say + something + TO + person:
    She said goodbye to him.
  • Fixed Phrases: say a word, say yes/no, say sorry, say hello, say thank you, say out loud, say a prayer.

TELL

Listener / Info

Focus on giving info. **Must have a listener object** (tell *someone*).

  • Tell + person + something:
    She told me the truth.
  • Tell + person + TO do:
    The teacher told us to study.
  • Fixed Phrases: tell a lie/the truth, tell a story/joke, tell the time, tell the difference, tell a secret.

SPEAK

Languages / Formal

Used for languages, speeches, 1-way delivery, and formal contexts.

  • Speak + Language:
    She speaks fluent Spanish.
  • Speak to/with + person:
    Can I speak to the manager?
  • Fixed Phrases: speak mind, speak highly of, speak publicly, generally speaking, speak up.

TALK

Chat / Informal

Focus on informal, 2-way interactive conversation between people.

  • Talk to/with + person:
    They talked with each other.
  • Talk about + topic:
    We talked about the movie.
  • Fixed Phrases: talk sense, talk nonsense, talk business, small talk, talk back, talk into/out of.
