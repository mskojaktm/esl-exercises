5Prepositional phrases & connectors
Replace the underlined idea with the correct preposition-based phrase. Target: stop overusing however and as a result.
1. The deadline was tight; however, we delivered on time. → the tight deadline, we delivered on time.
2. The new system is faster and also more secure. → being faster, the new system is more secure.
3. Concerning your question about salary, we'll discuss it next round. → your question about salary, we'll discuss it next round.
4. If the server fails, we have a backup ready. → a server failure, we have a backup ready.
5. The old code was messy; however, it worked reliably. → being messy, the old code worked reliably.
6. The outage was the cause of hours of data loss. → the outage, we lost several hours of data.

9Speaking production
Not scored — teacher-led. 30 sec prep, then 2 minutes uninterrupted.
Prompt: "Imagine you're in a job interview for a robotics engineering role in Europe. Describe a project you're proud of — what you were responsible for, what tools you're familiar with, and any challenges you dealt with despite tight deadlines."
  • At least 1 dependent preposition (responsible for / familiar with / involved in…)
  • At least 1 time or place preposition used correctly
  • At least 1 preposition-based connector (in spite of / despite / in terms of…)
  • Avoided defaulting to "however" or "as a result"
