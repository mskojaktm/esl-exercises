1 Warm-Up Chat (5 min)

Start relaxed — this mirrors the informal "meet the teachers" moment at BST. Goal: get him talking in full sentences before the harder tasks.

  • Tell me about your family — who's in it?
  • What do you usually do after school?
  • What's your favourite subject, and why?
  • Have you moved schools before? How did you feel?
💡 Don't correct grammar here — just get him comfortable speaking and used to open questions. Note down 2–3 weak spots to revisit later.

2 Verbal Reasoning Drill (20 min)

CAT4-style questions (Level D, ages 11–12) — the same reasoning style used in BST's entrance assessment. These test word relationships, not memorised facts, but a strong English vocabulary makes them much easier.

3 Spoken Interview Practice (15 min)
