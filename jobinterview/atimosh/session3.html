4Build Your Own STAR Story

Use a real project or situation. Keep Action as the most detailed part.

SSITUATION — Set the scene e.g. "During my final-year project, our team was building an autonomous robot for a competition."
TTASK — What were you responsible for? e.g. "I was responsible for the sensor integration and obstacle detection."
AACTION — What did you actually do? e.g. "I tested three sensor types, chose the most accurate and cost-effective one, and wrote the calibration code myself."
RRESULT — What happened? e.g. "As a result, our robot had the most reliable obstacle detection in the competition, and we placed in the top three."
