📚 Grammar Rules Reference

1. Basic Form & Spelling

  • Form: Subject + am / is / are + Verb-ing
  • She is reading a book.
  • Spelling -ing:
  • play → playing
  • make → making (drop silent -e)
  • lie → lying (-ie → -y)
  • run → running (double consonant)
Обычное употребление: действие прямо сейчас или в текущий период времени.

2. When to Use Continuous

  • Right now: I am typing an email right now.
  • Temporary / around now: He is reading "War and Peace".
  • Future arrangements: We are meeting tonight.
  • Annoying habits (+ always): She is always losing keys!
  • Changing situations: Your English is getting better.

3. Stative Verbs (No Continuous)

  • Emotions: love, hate, like, want, prefer, need
  • Mental: know, believe, understand, remember, forget
  • Possession: have (own), belong to, contain, consist of
Стативные глаголы выражают состояние, а не физическое действие. Они НЕ используются в Present Continuous!

4. Verbs with Dual Meanings (Simple vs. Continuous)

Verb Present Simple (State / Opinion / Quality) Present Continuous (Active / Temporary)
think I think he is kind. (opinion/believe) I am thinking about buying a bike. (considering right now)
have I have two cats. (possession) He is having lunch. (eating / activity)
see I see a bird. (perceive with eyes) I am seeing a doctor today. (meeting / appointment)
taste This soup tastes delicious. (has flavor) The chef is tasting the soup. (action of testing)
smell The rose smells sweet. (emits scent) The dog is smelling the shoes. (sniffing right now)
be You are silly. (permanent trait) You are being silly. (behaving foolishly now)
cost / weigh It costs $20 / weighs 5 kg. (fixed state) The clerk is weighing the box. (measuring action)
