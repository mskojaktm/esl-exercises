Quick rules
  • Form: had + V3. Negative: hadn’t + V3. Question: Had + subject + V3? Short answers: Yes, I had. / No, I hadn’t.
  • Past Perfect marks the earlier of two past actions. The later action, or a simple list of events in order, stays in the Past Simple.
  • Markers: by (the time), before, after, when, already, just, yet, never, hardly … when, no sooner … than.
  • Also used after thinking verbs, for reasons (because / as), in the third conditional, after wish / if only, and in reported speech.
  • Check your V3 forms: fly → flown, go → gone, give → given, draw → drawn, come → come, become → become.

Model answer

By the time Mia got home, her friends had already arrived. They had decorated the living room, and her sister had baked a cake. Mia hadn’t expected anything because she had told everyone she was too tired to celebrate. Hardly had she opened the door when everyone shouted “Surprise!” She wished she had changed her clothes before leaving the office, but nobody cared. If her sister hadn’t called the guests, the party would never have happened.
